## Platform

web

## Users

Real estate agency owners, managers and teams handling incoming enquiries and handing qualified prospects to sales agents.

## Product Purpose

Handle repetitive work between an incoming enquiry and a qualified prospect speaking with an agent: capture, respond, qualify, follow up, book and organise records.

## Positioning

A managed AI operations system between incoming enquiries and the sales team. Configured, launched and maintained around the client's existing workflow.

## Capabilities

AI voice agents, qualification, follow-up, missed call recovery, appointment booking, CRM sync, knowledge base, lead pipeline, call and activity tracking, automation analytics and operational reporting.
