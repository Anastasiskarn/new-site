We map how the work moves through your business, find where time is being lost, and improve the process with the right tools.

    Map

    Understand how the work happens today. We look at the steps, handoffs, tools and people involved in the process.

  2. 02

    Find the friction

    See where the process slows down. Manual work, repeated tasks, missed follow-ups, disconnected tools or poor visibility.

  3. 03

    Build

    Implement the right solution. That might be automation, software, AI, integrations, or a combination of them.

  4. 04

    Improve

    Keep the system useful as things change. We support and refine what we build as your team and processes evolve.

Frequently Asked Questions

The big picture

What AiAnchor does and how we think about solutions.

What does AiAnchor actually do?

We map how your business currently works, find where time or opportunities are being lost, and implement the right solution using automation, software, AI or a mix of them.

Do you only build AI solutions?

No. AI is one tool we use when it makes sense. Some problems are better solved with automation, integrations, dashboards or custom software.

Workflow review

How we understand the work before deciding what to change.

What does the workflow review include?

We look at how work moves through your team, which tools are involved, where handoffs break down, and which steps are taking unnecessary time.

Do you work with the tools we already use?

Yes. We normally build around your existing CRM, calendar, communication tools and internal systems rather than replacing everything.

The platform

How you track the systems, activity and results we implement.

What is the AiAnchor Operations Platform?

It gives you one place to track the systems we implement, including automations, AI agents, activity, leads, bookings and operational results.

Can I see whether the systems are actually working?

Yes. The platform is designed to give you visibility into activity, progress and the results generated by the systems running in your business.

Systems we build

The tools, agents and custom systems that can support your operation.

Can you build custom systems as well?

Yes. Depending on the problem, that can include internal tools, dashboards, portals, integrations and custom workflows.

Do you also build voice agents and chat assistants?

Yes, where they are useful for the workflow. They are part of the wider system rather than the entire offer.

Working together

Common use cases, scope, timing, support and human handoff.

Can AiAnchor automate lead handling and follow-up?

Yes. Lead capture, qualification, follow-up, missed-call recovery and appointment booking are common use cases.

Can you improve client onboarding and internal operations too?

Yes. The same process can be applied to onboarding, admin, reporting, handoffs and other repetitive workflows.

How does pricing work?

It depends on the workflow and what needs to be implemented. We normally review the process first, then scope the right level of work and support.

How long does setup take?

It depends on complexity. A focused workflow can be implemented much faster than a larger system involving several tools or departments.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. We can monitor, maintain and improve the systems after launch.

Can we start with one workflow first?

Yes. In many cases, starting with one clear process is the best way to prove the value before expanding further.

What happens if something needs a person?

We design the workflow so that human handoff happens at the right point, rather than trying to automate everything.
